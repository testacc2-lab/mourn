Quick answers. If you have more questions, join our Discord and ask away.

What is mourn.wtf?
mourn.wtf is a modern biolink platform that allows you to create and manage your own biolink pages. We also allow you to upload images and share them on Discord or anywhere you want with custom domain support.
How do I create a profile?
To create a profile, simply sign up and start creating your profile. Our user-friendly dashboard will guide you through the process.
How do I get premium?
To upgrade to premium, you can directly contact us on our Discord server and we'll guide you through the process, or go to mourn.wtf/pricing.
What integrations are available?
We have a few integrations available for you to use such as Discord and ShareX. However, you can link most of your social media accounts to your profile using the links page in the dashboard.
